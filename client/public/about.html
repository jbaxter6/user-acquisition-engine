About the company

Smooth Media Technologies LLC works with music artists and producers. We run live music review sessions that give them feedback and a way to build relationships with other artists and producers, grow their community, and earn from their music. We use Instagram and TikTok business accounts that we own and operate to stay in touch with the creators and listeners who contact us.

Our internal messaging tool

We operate more than one business account on each platform. To see and manage the direct message conversations across all of them, our team uses a private, internal inbox called the Unified Mailbox. It is used only by our own authorized staff. It is not a public product, it is not available for sign up, and it does not connect accounts that belong to other businesses.

How we use Instagram and TikTok

The tool connects only to business accounts owned by Smooth Media Technologies LLC, through each platform's official authorization flow and messaging API. It is used to:

  • view all of the direct message conversations on our connected accounts, including conversations that our team started from within the platform's own app and conversations that other people started
  • see the conversations from every account in one place, since we operate more than one business account on each platform
  • Only reply to messages from that one place
  • keep a record of each conversation for follow-up

The tool:

  • is not used for advertising
  • does not send automated, scheduled, or bulk messages through TikTok or Instagram
  • sends replies, on both Instagram and TikTok, only in conversations where the other person has already messaged us
  • does not access other people's accounts, and we do not sell or share platform data
