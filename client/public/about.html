What we do

Smooth Media Technologies LLC works with music artists and producers. We run live music review sessions that give them real feedback and a way to build relationships with other artists and producers, grow their community, and earn from their music. We use Instagram and TikTok business accounts to stay in touch with the creators and listeners who reach out to us.

The Smooth Unified Mailbox

When people message our business accounts, those messages used to be spread across separate apps and logins. The Mailbox brings them into one shared inbox so our team can respond quickly and consistently.

One shared inbox

Direct messages sent to our Instagram and TikTok business accounts appear together, with the full conversation history for each person.

Replies written by people

A team member reads each message and writes the reply. Messages are sent one at a time, never automatically or in bulk.

Consistent responses

Saved message templates help the team answer common questions the same way, and show which responses people actually reply to.

How we use Instagram and TikTok

The Mailbox connects only to business accounts owned and operated by Smooth Media Technologies LLC, using each platform's official authorization flow and messaging API.

What the Mailbox does

  • Shows us the direct messages people send to our connected business accounts
  • Lets a team member reply from the inbox
  • Keeps a record of each conversation for follow-up

What it does not do

  • It is not used for advertising
  • It does not send automated, scheduled, or bulk messages through TikTok
  • TikTok replies are sent only in conversations that the other person started
  • It does not access other people's accounts, and we never sell or share platform data

An internal tool

The Mailbox is used only by our own authorized team members. It is not open for public sign up and does not connect accounts that belong to other businesses. Access requires a password.
