Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smooth ("Smooth," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our use of the Instagram Messaging API to communicate with Instagram users who message our connected Instagram Business account(s).

This is an internal tool, not a public product. It is used only by Smooth's own authorized team members, signed in with Instagram Business accounts that Smooth controls and has explicitly connected. It is not available for other businesses or the general public to sign up for or use.

Throughout this policy, "team member" refers to Smooth's own authorized staff who use this tool. People who send messages to our connected Instagram accounts are not users of this tool. They are third parties whose message data we process as described below, in the course of a team member responding to them.

Information We Collect

When a person messages one of our connected Instagram accounts, we receive and store, about that person:

  • Their Instagram-scoped user ID (a unique identifier Meta assigns for that conversation, not their personal Instagram login or password)
  • Their Instagram username, where available
  • The content and timestamps of messages sent to and received from our connected account(s)

We do not collect a message sender's Instagram password, email, or phone number through this integration.

About our own team members, we store the Instagram Business account(s) they've connected (username, profile picture, and an access token Meta issues for that account), used solely to operate the tool.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Display incoming messages to team members and let them respond, in our internal messaging dashboard
  • Maintain a record of each conversation for support and follow-up purposes

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

How We Share Information

We do not share message data with third parties, except: (a) with service providers who host our infrastructure and are bound to keep it confidential, or (b) where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain conversation data for as long as reasonably necessary to operate our messaging inbox and respond to inquiries. A message sender may request deletion of their data at any time. See "Your Rights" below.

Data Security

Access tokens and message data are stored on infrastructure we control and are not made publicly accessible. Access is limited to Smooth's authorized team members operating the messaging inbox.

Your Rights

If you have sent a message to one of our connected Instagram accounts, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data we hold about that conversation by emailing support@smooth.fan. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@smooth.fan.
