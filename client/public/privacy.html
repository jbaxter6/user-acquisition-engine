Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smooth ("Smooth," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our use of the Instagram Messaging API to communicate with Instagram users who message our connected Instagram Business account(s).

Information We Collect

When you message one of our connected Instagram accounts, we receive and store:

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID (a unique identifier Meta assigns for our conversation with you — not your personal Instagram login or password)
  • Your Instagram username, where available
  • The content and timestamps of messages you send to and receive from our connected account(s)

We do not collect your Instagram password, email, or phone number through this integration.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Display and respond to your messages in our internal messaging dashboard
  • Maintain a record of our conversation with you for support and follow-up purposes

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

How We Share Information

We do not share your message data with third parties, except: (a) with service providers who host our infrastructure and are bound to keep it confidential, or (b) where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain conversation data for as long as reasonably necessary to operate our messaging inbox and respond to inquiries. You may request deletion of your data at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

Data Security

Access tokens and message data are stored on infrastructure we control and are not made publicly accessible. Access is limited to authorized personnel operating the messaging inbox.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data we hold about your conversations with us by emailing jbaxdevs@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to jbaxdevs@gmail.com.
