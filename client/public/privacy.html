Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smooth Media Technologies LLC ("Smooth," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Smooth Unified Mailbox (the "Mailbox"), our internal messaging inbox. The Mailbox uses the Instagram Messaging API (provided by Meta) and the TikTok Business Messaging API (provided by TikTok) so that our team can read and reply to direct messages sent to the Instagram Business and TikTok Business accounts that we own and operate.

The Mailbox is an internal tool, not a public product. It is used only by Smooth's own authorized team members, who connect Instagram and TikTok business accounts that Smooth controls. It is not available for other businesses or the general public to sign up for or use, and we do not authorize or connect accounts that belong to third parties.

Throughout this policy, "team member" refers to Smooth's own authorized staff who use the Mailbox. People who send messages to our connected Instagram or TikTok accounts are not users of the Mailbox. They are third parties whose message data we process as described below, in the course of a team member responding to them.

Information We Collect

From Instagram

When a person messages one of our connected Instagram accounts, we receive and store, about that person:

  • Their Instagram-scoped user ID (a unique identifier Meta assigns for that conversation, not their personal Instagram login or password)
  • Their Instagram username and profile picture, where Meta makes them available
  • The content and timestamps of messages sent to and received from our connected account(s)

From TikTok

When a person messages one of our connected TikTok Business accounts, we receive and store, about that person and the conversation:

  • The identifier TikTok assigns to that person for the conversation, and their username or display name, where TikTok provides them
  • The content and timestamps of messages sent to and received from our connected account(s)

This includes conversations that our team members started from within TikTok's own app, which appear in the Mailbox alongside conversations that other people started, so that our team can see the full history of each connected account in one place. We operate more than one business account on each platform.

What we do not collect

We do not collect a message sender's Instagram or TikTok password, email address, or phone number through these integrations, and we do not access their followers, videos, likes, or any account activity beyond the conversation they have with our account.

About our team members and connected accounts

For each Instagram or TikTok account a team member connects, we store the account's username or ID, profile picture where available, and the access token that Meta or TikTok issues for that account. These are used solely to operate the Mailbox. Team members sign in to the Mailbox with a password, and their session is kept in a signed, HTTP-only cookie.

Information our team records about creators and brands

Our team also keeps a list of creators and brands we work with. For each, this can include a public handle, display name, follower count, public business contact details, and internal notes and message templates that our team writes. This information comes from public profiles, from lists our team imports, and from the username of anyone who messages one of our connected accounts.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Display incoming messages to team members and let them respond, in our internal messaging dashboard
  • Maintain a record of each conversation for support and follow-up purposes
  • Understand which of our own message templates receive replies

Data received through the Instagram and TikTok messaging APIs is used only to provide these functions. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, we do not use it to train machine-learning or AI models, and we do not sell, rent, or license it to anyone.

Messages We Send

Messages sent through the Mailbox's Instagram and TikTok integrations are written and sent one at a time by a team member, and only in conversations where the other person has already messaged us. The Mailbox does not send automated, scheduled, or bulk messages, and it does not use the Instagram or TikTok APIs to contact people who have not messaged us first.

How We Share Information

We do not share message data with third parties, except: (a) with service providers who host our infrastructure and are bound to keep it confidential, or (b) where required by law. We do not sell or transfer Instagram or TikTok data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain conversation data for as long as reasonably necessary to operate our messaging inbox and respond to inquiries. When a team member disconnects an Instagram or TikTok account from the Mailbox, the stored access token for that account is deleted and the Mailbox can no longer read or send messages for it. A message sender may request deletion of their data at any time. See "Your Rights" below.

Data Security

Access tokens and message data are stored on infrastructure we control and are not made publicly accessible. Access to the Mailbox is limited to Smooth's authorized team members and requires a password. Connections to the Mailbox use HTTPS.

Revoking Access

Access we hold for a connected account can be removed at any time. A team member can disconnect the account inside the Mailbox, or the account owner can remove our app's access from the connected account's app or website permissions settings on Instagram or TikTok. After access is removed, we no longer receive new messages for that account.

Your Rights

If you have sent a message to one of our connected Instagram or TikTok accounts, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data we hold about that conversation by emailing support@smooth.fan. Please include the platform and the username you messaged from so we can find it. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Third-Party Platforms

Instagram is operated by Meta and TikTok is operated by TikTok. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your information on their platforms. We use their APIs in accordance with their developer terms and platform policies.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we have received data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
